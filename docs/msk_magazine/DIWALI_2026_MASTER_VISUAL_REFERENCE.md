## Design rules
- Premium financial-magazine aesthetic, not a slide deck or school report. Use strong editorial typography, white space, restrained navy/green/teal accents and gold for selected emphasis; reserve red for genuine risk/negative values.
- Use consistent header, page number, footer, margins and visual grid. Use headline/deck hierarchy, not repetitive boxed paragraphs. Fit charts and infographics to the editorial point.
- Every chart must be readable, accurately scaled, labeled and based on verified dated data. Missing evidence must not be replaced with fabricated charts or signals.
