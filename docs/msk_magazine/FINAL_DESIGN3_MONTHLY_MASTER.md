## Monthly changes only

* Cover: change issue/month and teasers, not original design or MSK logo.
* Index: change monthly editor's note and month, **not index entries or page numbers**.
* Research pages 03–15: refresh article, actual dated sources, verified figures/charts, market/issuer analysis and risk callouts.
* Page 13: a **new** Strategy Lab topic, not repeated editorial copy.
* Page 16: update actual cited sources, evidence status, disclosures and any missing-data limitations.
* Keep visual balance of roughly **50% analysis, 30% charts/infographics, 20% insights/data**, without fabricating empty-data charts.

## Page-by-page workflow (each month)

1. Initialize a fresh `YYYY-MM` issue with 16 pages all **PENDING**.
2. Work on **one page at a time**, preserving its frozen title, number and Design 3 visual layout.
3. Re-source financial figures and verify calculations. Never reuse November 2026 market prices, CPI, company financials, chart data or future event dates as current.
4. Produce one A4 page PDF, compare visually with the matching Design 3 reference page, check logo, grid, text clipping, charts and dated sources.
5. Record source date, source IDs, PDF checksum, reviewer's content/visual checks. No automated sign-off based on tests alone.
6. Assemble exactly 16 reviewed pages as **EDITORIAL DRAFT**. Missing pages or review proof must block final assembly.
7. Separately secure real source QA, editorial sign-off, final 16-page visual review, owner approval and Telegram delivery proof before publication.
