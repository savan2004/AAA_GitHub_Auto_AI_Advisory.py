## October inflation: pressure increased

MoSPI's October 2026 CPI release (provisional, 12 November) reports combined headline inflation at **6.09%**, versus **5.54%** for September (final), a **0.55 percentage-point increase**. Food inflation rose to **8.94%** from **7.74%**, up **1.20 points**. October rural CPI inflation was **6.63%**, versus **5.41%** urban, a 1.22-point gap. Division rates include 8.51% for food and beverages, 10.06% for restaurants/accommodation, and 10.14% for personal care/social protection.

## Activity and monetary policy

The September 2026 IIP release (28 October) reports **6.7% year-on-year** growth, compared with August's **8.0% quick estimate**. Manufacturing grew **7.6%** and electricity/gas supply **11.2%**. These quick estimates may be revised.

RBI's October 2026 policy summary reports a **25-basis-point repo increase to 5.50%** on 7 October and a calibrated-tightening stance. The eSankhyiki portal currently displays **Q1 FY2026-27 GDP growth of 7.8%**.

## Editorial interpretation (not a forecast)

Positive GDP and industrial output remain supportive of activity. However, the rise in headline and food inflation, the rural–urban price gap, and a tighter policy stance argue against assuming easy liquidity. Track whether food inflation eases, industrial momentum stabilises, and policy communication changes. This is a risk framing only; it is not a stock selection or investment recommendation.
